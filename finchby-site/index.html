Client story

A respected fabricator that AI couldn't see

A long-established stone fabricator in Central Texas had strong reviews and industry credentials, yet almost never came up when people asked AI for the best in the city.

The problem wasn't the quality of the business. Two websites and two phone numbers were in circulation, three different founding years were quoted online, and the list sites AI relied on had the details wrong. No page stated prices, terms or credentials.

We're fixing it in order: one master record, the core profiles, the cited list sites, then the website, with a monthly re-test against the baseline.
