The Firm

About Finchby

Finchby is an independent consultancy in AI visibility and website improvement, serving established local businesses. It is conducted personally by its principal, and takes on a small number of clients at a time.

A traditional practice in a new field

The field is new and changes monthly; the manner of practice need not be. Finchby is run as a professional firm of the older kind: the work is done carefully, explained plainly, and judged by its results.

We do not make loud promises. We record where a client stands, do the work in order, and show the figures afterwards. Clients are gained by performance and kept by trust.

The principal

Steven Hope founded Finchby after advising a business owner on why his long-established firm was missing from AI answers, and finding that the remedy was mostly orderly, unglamorous work that few were offering to do properly.

He studies each new AI model and platform as it is released, and brings into the practice only what has proved itself in a client's figures.

Principles

  • Evidence first. A baseline before any advice.
  • Candour. Including what not to buy, and what cannot be promised.
  • Order. Foundations before advertising.
  • Stewardship. The client owns every account, record and page.
  • Discretion. Clients are not named without their leave.

The house device

The arms and the finch

The finch is a small, quick and exact bird, and stands for the manner in which the firm works. It appears in flight as the firm's mark and in the base of the shield.

The chevron and roundels are drawn from the arms of Hope, and the firm's regard for tenacity from the Leslie motto, Grip Fast; both are Scottish families of the principal's descent.

The motto, Fide et Opera, means “by trust and by work.”

The device is a house mark adopted by the firm, and not a grant of arms.
