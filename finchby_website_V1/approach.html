How it works

A clear process, in the right order

We measure what AI says about your business, fix the sources it reads, upgrade your website, and re-test every month. Order matters: advertising a business whose facts don't match just sends buyers to a contradiction.

1

Measure

We put real customer questions to each AI engine, several times, and record the answers: whether you're named, what's said, which sources are cited and which competitors appear. That's your baseline.

2

Agree the facts

One short meeting settles the basics: your exact name, address, phone, hours, founding year, credentials and terms. That becomes your master record.

3

Fix the sources

We claim and complete your core profiles, then correct or join the directories and list sites AI cites. Everything is checked against the master record.

4

Upgrade the website

We clear the clutter, add the labels machines read, and write the missing pages. Each one answers its question in the first two sentences, with real prices, lead times and dates.

5

Build reputation

A routine for asking every customer for a review and replying to each. If AI repeats an unfair claim, we publish the real facts and respond at the source.

6

Track monthly

We re-run the same questions and compare with the baseline, alongside data from Google, Bing and your own enquiry form. The plan adjusts based on the numbers.

What makes us different

We tell you what not to buy

Every Finchby plan includes a list of things not to spend time or money on. Good advice includes what to leave alone.

The list changes as AI platforms change, and we update it based on evidence. Right now it usually includes the items here.

  • Paid “best of” badges on sites that just copy their data from Google and Yelp.
  • Buying, filtering or rewarding reviews. It risks your profile being suspended.
  • Trendy technical files that no major AI engine currently reads.
  • Daily tracking subscriptions before a monthly check shows a gap worth watching.
  • Ads before your foundations are consistent.
  • Content hidden in tabs, scripts or PDFs, where AI crawlers can't read it.

FAQ

Common questions

Can you guarantee ChatGPT will recommend my business?

No, and nobody honestly can. The same question gives different businesses from one run to the next. What we can do is raise how often you appear and make sure the facts are right. We measure both.

How is this different from SEO?

It includes SEO, but the weight is elsewhere. AI engines lean heavily on third-party sources like maps data, reviews, directories and list sites, so much of the work happens off your website.

How long until I see results?

Corrected listings and profiles can be picked up by AI within weeks. New website pages and a body of reviews take longer. We plan in 90-day blocks and check monthly.

Do I need a new website?

Rarely. Most sites need a cleanup, proper labelling and a handful of new pages. We work in WordPress with Elementor and Yoast SEO, alongside your developer if you have one.

Who does the work?

Finchby's founder, using AI research tools that he directs and checks. Some tasks, like gathering facts, photos and reviews, need the owner. The plan says exactly which.

What kinds of businesses do you work with?

Established local businesses whose customers ask “who should I call?”: trades, contractors, fabricators, showrooms and professional practices.
