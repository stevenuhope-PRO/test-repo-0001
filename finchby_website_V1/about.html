About

Modern work, old-fashioned standards

Finchby is an independent AI visibility consultancy for local businesses. It's run personally by its founder and works with a small number of clients at a time.

Why Finchby exists

Founder Steven Hope started Finchby after helping a business owner work out why his long-established company was missing from AI answers. The fix turned out to be mostly careful, unglamorous work that few people were offering to do properly.

AI platforms change every month. Steven tests each new model and tool as it's released, and brings into client work only what proves itself in real results.

The name is deliberate. It's meant to sound like a firm that's been around for a century, because that's the standard we hold ourselves to: do the work carefully, explain it clearly, and earn the next client through results.

What we stand for

  • Evidence first. A baseline before any advice.
  • Honesty. Including what not to buy and what can't be promised.
  • Order. Foundations before advertising.
  • Ownership. You keep every account, record and page.
  • Discretion. We don't name clients without permission.

The crest

Where the finch comes from

The finch is small, quick and precise, which is how we like to work. It's our logo, and it sits at the top of the crest.

The shield is divided into three parts, one for each value we work by. The owl stands for wisdom, the wing for agility as AI changes, and the anchor for integrity: advice you can trust to hold steady.

The rest borrows from the founder's Scottish family heritage. The chevron comes from the arms of Hope, and the three buckles come from Leslie. The laurel branches are the long-standing mark of work judged on merit.

The crest is a company emblem inspired by those family arms, not an official grant of arms.
