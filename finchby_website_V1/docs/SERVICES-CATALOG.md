## 2. What is distinctive in the Victory work

These are uncommon in the market and are written into the site as Finchby's character:

1. **Source Tracing.** Most agencies optimize the client's website. The Victory plan found the
   engines took their "best in Austin" answers from four third-party list sites plus Google/Yelp data,
   then went after those sources. This is the strongest differentiator.
2. **Master Record.** One agreed fact sheet, with every listing checked against it field by field.
   Agencies talk about "NAP consistency"; few produce a named, owned artefact.
3. **Duplicate and Legacy Cleanup.** Two live sites, a locked domain held by a former owner,
   two phone numbers, three founding years. Working *around* what cannot be changed is real consulting.
4. **AI Answer Corrections.** Tracing an unfavourable AI claim (the deposit allegation) to its source,
   publishing true terms, and replying at the source. Few firms offer this for AI answers.
5. **Repeated answer checks.** Each prompt run three times per engine because answers repeat only
   21–33% of the time. Most "AI audits" are a single run.
6. **A "do not spend" list in every plan.** llms.txt, paid badges, premature tracking tools, ads before
   foundations. Candour as a selling point; it has its own section on the Approach page.
7. **A plain verdict audit** (Ready / Needs Work / Blocked) from your own script, re-run after each change.
8. **Owner-readable plans** with every task assigned to a named person, time and cost.
9. **Measuring the unmeasurable:** the "How did you hear about us?" question with AI options, the GA4
   "AI assistants" channel, and Bing's AI Performance report.
10. **Host-level crawler access** (Cloudflare AI-bot settings), which most SEO checklists miss.
11. **Neglected profiles** (Apple Business, Bing Places) treated as first-class.
12. **AI-directed research.** Multi-agent research under your supervision lets one principal do the
    work of a small team. The site says this plainly on the Approach page.
